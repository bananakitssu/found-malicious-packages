## Summary

Malicious data exfiltration and privilege escalation in aiosendletter

## Details

The PyPI package aiosendletter is malware. It masquerades as a 'bug reporter' / utility but, on execution, attempts UAC elevation via ctypes.windll.shell32.ShellExecuteW with the 'runas' verb, archives local data (including %LOCALAPPDATA%\logs and, in related campaign packages, Telegram Desktop tdata), and POSTs the archive to a hardcoded Cloudflare Workers endpoint (sparkling-pine-f202.stilluer-sweden.workers.dev). The package has been removed from PyPI. Independent confirmations: Snyk SNYK-PYTHON-AIOSENDLETTER-19920809, OpenSSF/OSSF malicious-packages, Corgea MAL-2026-16264. Campaign also includes related packages such as pullgetsage targeting Telegram session data.
